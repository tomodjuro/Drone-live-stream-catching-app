Instructions:

* This method, for receiving a live stream, can work without mobile data (internet) and can be used in forests or other remote locations without signal; however, if a signal is available, using mobile data is recommended.
  If operating without mobile data, the live stream may drop occasionally as the laptop automatically searches for a Wi-Fi connection with internet access. If this happens, simply restart the live stream on the controller and it will resume working.

1. TURN ON MOBILE HOTSPOT on your phone and connect both your laptop and controller to it. (This applies if you have the RC 2 controller; for the RC N2 controller, connect using the mobile device attached to it).
   * Note for RC 2 controller users: You must plug an audio device with a microphone into the USB-C port of the controller, otherwise the live stream will not start.

* Before running the application Dron_stream_catcher(.exe), you may need to allow it through your Firewall and/or Antivirus software (create an exception), or do so when prompted by the system after launching the app.

2. LAUNCH THE APPLICATION Dron_stream_catcher.exe

A window will open displaying your laptop's IP address and the RTMP stream address that you will enter into the controller menu later.
Verify that the green status light for MonaServer is ON. If not, click "Start MonaServer".

3. POWER ON THE DRONE AND CONTROLLER, open the DJI Fly app on the controller, and tap "Go Fly" (if it hasn't launched automatically).

4. ONCE THE CAMERA FEED APPEARS on the controller screen, tap the three dots (...) in the top right corner to open the menu.

   Go to the Transmission tab.

   Tap Live Streaming Platforms and select RTMP.

   Enter the exact RTMP URL format in the input field.

   Correct syntax format:
   rtmp://[LAPTOP_LOCAL_IP_ADDRESS]:1935/live/mystream

Option A: If you have a single input field (RTMP URL)

Combine everything into a single line separated by slash (/):

   RTMP URL: rtmp://[LAPTOP_IP_ADDRESS]:1935/live/mystream

Option B: If you have two separate input fields

Enter the address as follows:

   RTMP URL: rtmp://[LAPTOP_IP_ADDRESS]:1935/live/

   Stream Key: mystream

(The stream key "mystream" is arbitrary – you can type "drone", "test", "123", or any string of your choice).

5. CONFIRM THE ADDRESS by tapping the blue button at the bottom right after typing the address, then tap "Start". A countdown will begin, and a small orange icon will appear on the controller screen, indicating that the stream is active and working properly.

6. RETURN TO THE LAPTOP APPLICATION and check if the green status light for "Drone Stream Status" is ON. If it is green, the live stream has started successfully.

7. IN THE APPLICATION, CLICK "Open Live Stream". After a few seconds of buffer/processing, a window with an embedded video player will open. Once finished, simply click the small "X" button at the top right of the window to close it.

---

* ALTERNATIVE STREAM VIEWERS:

 - VLC Media Player:

   Open VLC Media Player.

   Click Media -> Open Network Stream.

   Enter the exact same address you entered in the drone: rtmp://[LAPTOP_LOCAL_IP_ADDRESS]:1935/live/mystream

   Click Play.

 - YouTube Live Stream:

   On your computer, open and log into YouTube. Click your profile icon -> YouTube Studio -> Create -> Go Live.
   This opens the live streaming setup window displaying your RTMP stream URL and Stream Key, which can be entered into the DJI Fly app on the controller in the same way.

---

* TROUBLESHOOTING / If the stream does not work:

Windows by default blocks incoming traffic on unknown ports when connected to a mobile hotspot (as it identifies it as a "Public network").

   On your laptop, open Windows Defender Firewall -> Advanced Settings.

   Click Inbound Rules, then click New Rule.

   Select Port, click Next, select TCP, and enter the port used by MonaServer: 1935.

   Click Next, select "Allow the connection", and complete the wizard by naming the rule (e.g., "MonaServer").

If configured correctly, the MonaServer log window on your laptop will show that the connection has been established successfully.
